# Account and outbound operations

This reference grants no account access, sending, publication, spending or scheduling.
Resolve exact current account/action envelopes and do not ask again for covered effects.
Keep identities, credential references and account-specific facts in their existing private
owners—not public skill text, templates or logs. Aliases do not create independent sender
reputation; additional addresses do not establish recipient eligibility or permission.

## Readiness and admission

1. Define the product/audience/channel hypothesis, eligible recipient provenance and relevant
   jurisdiction/provider rules. Transactional, subscribed marketing and cold outreach have
   distinct requirements; email copy is not deliverability or authorization.
2. Verify truthful sender identity, provider authentication/alignment, reply routing, bounce/
   complaint events, suppression/unsubscribe and quotas. Domain/IP reputation can aggregate
   across infrastructure; mailbox rotation does not reset it.
3. Bind account/cohort/time/volume/cost limits, health thresholds and suppression ownership
   to the actual grant. Prepare a synthetic/redacted dry run for review before a live effect
   when required by that envelope. Evidence-only planning does not authenticate or send.
4. At the supported actuator, recheck policy, exact account/grant revisions and suppression
   immediately before the effect. Persist operation/idempotency key and provider result.
5. Reconcile ambiguous timeouts before retrying. Verify event signatures on original bytes,
   durably accept before acknowledgment, and dedupe/claim atomically. Check SDK error shape.
6. Measure qualified replies, activated users, total cost and complaints over the declared
   window. Privacy/scanner-distorted opens alone do not prove useful distribution.

Ramp legitimate, relevant traffic within provider limits using real feedback and healthy
observation windows. Never manufacture opens/replies or rotate identities to evade restrictions.
Suppressed/duplicate recipients, stale evidence, revoked authority, cooldown, sparse samples,
unhealthy senders or full queues select no-op/defer. Pause only the owned campaign when its
exact stop contract applies; do not change unrelated services. Retain recoverable progress.

## Primary guidance and complements

[Google sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126?hl=en) and
[bulk-sender FAQ](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14229414?hl=en) were inspected on
2026-10-04. Recheck current guidance before sending. Gmail distinguishes all senders from
bulk senders; bulk requirements include SPF and DKIM, DMARC, and one-click unsubscribe for
marketing/subscribed messages. Its recommendation is spam below 0.1%, avoiding 0.3% or
higher. These are Gmail requirements, not universal admission thresholds.

[Resend skills](../SOURCES.md#resendresend-skills--resend) are an optional operational
reference, not an installed sender. The same durability/concurrency/authorization checks
apply to any provider. Compliance and current account authority must be established by
the real sending owner; this generic reference cannot certify either.
