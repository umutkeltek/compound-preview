# research branch

Load for the actual research task. Use the [common execution contract](../README.md#common-execution-contract); this file grants no action authority.

## Trigger and input

A new customer observation, interview or unmet-need question.
Input: Current audience/task, observed behavior, raw sources, sample and consent limits.

## Procedure and composition

Separate observed problems from proposed solutions. Compare at least two explanations when evidence permits; seek disconfirming examples. Preserve contradictions, unknowns and sample selection. An unreleased product with no telemetry starts with bounded task observation, not invented cohorts.
Optional complement: customer-research methods for actual customer/user evidence. Verify installation, provenance and its narrower contract before loading. Do not import an external body merely because this branch names it.

## Output and proof

A decision-ready problem/evidence brief with source revisions and unresolved assumptions.
Next context: strategy or experiment, once problem/audience evidence is usable. Record source/entity revision, falsifier, uncertainty and named receiving owner; require actual acknowledgment/consumption before claiming handoff. See the [source catalog](../SOURCES.md) for conceptual credits, not efficacy evidence.

## Silence and recovery

Do not invent respondents, quotes or demand. Contacting people needs its own grant; an unrelated interview stays out.
If sources fail, preserve retrieved evidence and stop unsupported conclusions; retry only transient reads.
Duplicate/stale events, unavailable capacity/consumer, immature windows or a missing grant select no-op, bounded observation or explicit defer. A routing offer proves none of execution, recovery, operator-time savings or business impact.
