# strategy branch

Load for the actual strategy task. Use the [common execution contract](../README.md#common-execution-contract); this file grants no action authority.

## Trigger and input

An audience, promise, differentiation or pricing decision.
Input: Problem evidence, alternatives, business constraints, resources and current positioning.

## Procedure and composition

Choose a narrow target and credible value promise. Explain why an alternative would win or lose. Compare product/business theses, not slogans. Define the falsifier and opportunity cost; identify which price/packaging assumptions need evidence rather than changing them.
Optional complement: pricing and marketing-context methods only for the touched business seam. Verify installation, provenance and its narrower contract before loading. Do not import an external body merely because this branch names it.

## Output and proof

Positioning/strategy brief tied to observed need and a declared decision criterion.
Next context: experiment, UX or spec, after the actual strategy choice is resolved. Record source/entity revision, falsifier, uncertainty and named receiving owner; require actual acknowledgment/consumption before claiming handoff. See the [source catalog](../SOURCES.md) for conceptual credits, not efficacy evidence.

## Silence and recovery

No invented competitive wins. A brief does not authorize price, billing or store changes.
Keep rejected theses and unresolved constraints so new evidence can reopen the decision without restarting research.
Duplicate/stale events, unavailable capacity/consumer, immature windows or a missing grant select no-op, bounded observation or explicit defer. A routing offer proves none of execution, recovery, operator-time savings or business impact.
