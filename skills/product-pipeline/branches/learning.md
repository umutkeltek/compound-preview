## Procedure and composition

Read ../references/skill-selection.md. Find the next real consumer; mechanize repeated checks at their deterministic owner. Update judgment only for a demonstrated gap. Optional complement: verified skill-authoring/evaluation tooling; no bulk repo installation. Verify installation, provenance and its narrower contract before loading. Do not import an external body merely because this branch names it.

## Output and proof

Next context: router context for the next bounded branch; actual authoring/check owner must consume the delta. Record source/entity revision, falsifier, uncertainty and named receiving owner; require actual acknowledgment/consumption before claiming handoff. See the [source catalog](../SOURCES.md) for conceptual credits, not efficacy evidence.

## Silence and recovery

No self-promotion after popularity, one success, declared-use labels or a prettier screenshot.
Keep prior bytes and failed cases; revert or park a harmful change through its owner rather than weakening evaluation gates.
Duplicate/stale events, unavailable capacity/consumer, immature windows or a missing grant select no-op, bounded observation or explicit defer. A routing offer proves none of execution, recovery, operator-time savings or business impact.
