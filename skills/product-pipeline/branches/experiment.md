# experiment branch

Load for the actual experiment task. Use the [common execution contract](../README.md#common-execution-contract); this file grants no action authority.

## Trigger and input

A testable hypothesis and decision window need design.
Input: Baseline/unknowns, eligible population, metric denominator, allocation, sample feasibility and guardrails.

## Procedure and composition

Predeclare hypothesis, intervention, comparison, stopping rules and decision threshold. Distinguish exploration from a confirmatory test. Account for seasonality, novelty and selection; do not improvise significance. Check existing tests to avoid conflicting interventions.
Optional complement: experiment/statistics expertise when the design needs it. Verify installation, provenance and its narrower contract before loading. Do not import an external body merely because this branch names it.

## Output and proof

One bounded experiment/observation plan with a falsifier, measurement contract and owner.
Next context: spec for implementation; measurement once instrumentation and the window are ready. Record source/entity revision, falsifier, uncertainty and named receiving owner; require actual acknowledgment/consumption before claiming handoff. See the [source catalog](../SOURCES.md) for conceptual credits, not efficacy evidence.

## Silence and recovery

No fabricated power, automatic spending, active-test duplication or early win claims. Sparse evidence can justify more observation, not success.
Persist assignments/version and partial data. Do not silently change allocation or stopping criteria after seeing results.
Duplicate/stale events, unavailable capacity/consumer, immature windows or a missing grant select no-op, bounded observation or explicit defer. A routing offer proves none of execution, recovery, operator-time savings or business impact.
